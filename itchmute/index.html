ItchMute

ItchMute is a free browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that lets you hide games, developers and tags on itch.io, which does not offer an ignore option.

What it does

  • Adds an "Ignore" button to game cards and game pages on itch.io, and another one for developers.
  • Hides the ignored games and developers from browse, search and profile lists, either removed or replaced by a small notice.
  • Keeps your lists on your device. Export and import them as a file at any time.
  • Optional sync with your own Google Drive, so your lists follow you across devices. It uses only the hidden app data folder (drive.appdata scope): the extension cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Privacy

ItchMute has no server, no analytics and no ads. The developer receives no data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
