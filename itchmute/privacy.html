ItchMute: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

ItchMute is a browser extension that lets you hide games and developers on itch.io.

Data we collect

None. ItchMute does not collect, transmit or share personal data or browsing data with the developer. The developer runs no server and receives nothing.

Data stored on your device

The extension stores only its own data in your browser (chrome.storage.local):

  • the list of games you chose to ignore (id, title, link and developer name),
  • the list of developers you chose to ignore (name and link),
  • whether hiding is on or off,
  • how ignored items are shown (a notice or removed),
  • the selected language.

This data stays on your device, except when you export it yourself to a file, or when you choose to sync it with your Google Drive (see below). It is removed when you uninstall the extension.

Optional sync with Google Drive

Syncing is off by default and only happens when you press its button in the settings page. When you do, Google asks you to sign in and grant the extension access to one scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This scope only gives access to a hidden folder reserved for the extension in your own Google Drive. The extension cannot see, list or change any other file in your Drive, and it does not read your name, email or any other Google account data.

The extension saves one file in that folder with the same lists described above (ignored games and developers) and reads it back on your other devices. The data travels directly between your browser and Google. It is not sent to the developer or to any other third party.

You can revoke the access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the file from the same place. Google's own privacy policy applies to data stored in your Drive.

ItchMute's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the extension accesses

The extension runs only on itch.io and its subdomains. There it reads the game cards and pages (game id, title, link and developer) to hide the ones in your list and to add its buttons. It does not read, store or send your account details, your purchases or any other page content.

Third parties

ItchMute uses no analytics, advertising or cookies. The only network requests it makes are to Google, and only when you use the optional sync described above.

The settings page includes an optional link to Ko-fi (ko-fi.com). The extension sends nothing to Ko-fi. If you open the link, Ko-fi's own privacy policy applies to that site.

Affiliation

ItchMute is an independent project. It is not affiliated with itch.io.
